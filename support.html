Frequently Asked Questions

How many birthdays can I save?

CakeDayAlerts lets you save up to five birthdays for free. You can unlock unlimited birthdays with a one-time in-app purchase.

Is the unlimited unlock a subscription?

No! Unlimited birthdays require just one payment. There are no recurring subscription charges.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open CakeDayAlerts, navigate to Settings, and select Restore Purchases.

Make sure you're using the Apple Account associated with your original purchase.

Why am I not receiving birthday notifications?

Open your iPhone Settings, select Notifications, find CakeDayAlerts, and enable Allow Notifications.

Also check your Focus settings and confirm that reminders are enabled for the birthday in the app.

Can I customize reminders?

Yes. Open a saved birthday to configure its reminder options.

Available reminder options depend on your installed app version.

How do I import birthdays from my contacts?

Open the birthday import feature in CakeDayAlerts and grant Contacts access when prompted.

Select the contacts containing birthdays that you want to import.

Only contacts with birthday information can be imported.

How do I create a birthday card?

Open a saved birthday and select the option to create a birthday card.

Choose a design, customize the text, adjust the appearance, and add a photo if desired.

Preview your creation before sharing it.

How do I send a birthday card?

After creating your birthday card, select the sharing option.

Choose Messages, Mail, or another compatible application.

CakeDayAlerts creates an image of your card that you can share.

Why can't I save birthday cards to Photos?

Check that CakeDayAlerts has permission to add photos to your photo library.

You can manage photo permissions in your iPhone's Privacy & Security settings.

If the problem continues, contact support with your iOS version.

How do I add birthday widgets?

Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen.

Select the option to add a widget, search for CakeDayAlerts, and choose an available widget size.

Widget availability depends on your installed app version and device compatibility.

Can I back up my birthdays?

Open Settings and select the birthday export option.

Save your backup somewhere secure.

If your installed version supports backup importing, you can restore your exported data.

Will deleting the app erase my birthdays?

Deleting the app may permanently remove birthdays stored locally on your device.

Export a backup before deleting CakeDayAlerts or changing devices.

Still Need Help?

If you're experiencing crashes, purchase problems, notification issues, or have a feature suggestion, we'd love to hear from you.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.
