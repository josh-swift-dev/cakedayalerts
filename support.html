CakeDayAlerts Support 🎂

Need help with birthdays, reminders, cards, or purchases? Here are answers to common questions.

How do I add birthdays?

Open CakeDayAlerts and select Add Birthday. Enter their name and birthday, then save the entry.

Why aren't reminders appearing?

In the app's Settings, select Enable Notifications. If iOS permission was previously denied, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → CakeDayAlerts and allow notifications.

Check reminder options for the birthday and reopen the app to refresh scheduled notifications. iOS may limit the number of pending local notifications.

How do I create and send a birthday card?

Open a birthday and select Make a Birthday Card. Customize the card and use its share option to send the resulting picture through a supported app. You choose the recipient and confirm sending.

Why can't I save cards to Photos?

Check your device's Photos permission for CakeDayAlerts. If saving still fails, restart the app and try again.

Include your iOS version and a description of the problem when contacting support.

How does unlimited work?

The free version allows five saved birthdays. The optional one-time purchase removes that limit. No subscription is required.

To recover an eligible prior purchase, use Restore Purchases in Settings while signed in to the Apple Account used for purchase.

How do I keep my birthdays when changing phones?

Use the app's backup export feature to save your information. If your installed version supports backup import, import that saved file on the new device.

Keep the backup private because it may contain names, dates, and notes.
