Privacy Policy

Your birthdays are personal. CakeDayAlerts is designed to help you remember important celebrations while keeping your information private.

1. Information You Provide

CakeDayAlerts allows you to save information such as:

  • Names and birthdays
  • Profile pictures
  • Relationships and personal notes
  • Gift ideas and gift history
  • Birthday card messages and photos
  • Reminder preferences

This information is used to provide the features of the application.

2. Data Storage

Birthday information is stored locally on your device.

CakeDayAlerts does not require an account or operate its own server for storing your birthday information.

Information may also be included in device backups managed by Apple. If you export or share information, its handling will depend on the destination you choose.

3. Contacts Access

With your permission, CakeDayAlerts can access your contacts to import names and birthdays.

Contact information is used for birthday importing and is not uploaded to our servers.

You can manage Contacts access through your device settings.

4. Photos

You may choose photos for birthday profiles or personalized birthday cards.

Selected photos are used for the features you request.

If you save a birthday card to your photo library, CakeDayAlerts will request the necessary permission from iOS.

5. Notifications

CakeDayAlerts uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming birthdays.

You can disable notifications or change notification permissions in your device settings.

6. Birthday Cards and Sharing

CakeDayAlerts allows you to create and share personalized birthday cards.

Cards are shared only when you initiate a sharing action.

Once you share a card through Messages, email, or another service, that service's privacy policy applies.

7. Purchases

CakeDayAlerts offers five free birthday entries and an optional one-time purchase to unlock unlimited birthdays.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment card information.

8. Advertising and Analytics

CakeDayAlerts does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

9. Data Export and Deletion

You can edit or delete saved birthdays within the application.

CakeDayAlerts also provides birthday data export and backup-import features.

Deleting the application generally removes its locally stored data, although exported files and device backups may remain.

10. Children's Privacy

CakeDayAlerts is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information through developer-operated servers.

Users should obtain appropriate permission before storing or sharing another person's personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change.

Updates will be published on this page.
