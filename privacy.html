CakeDayAlerts Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

CakeDayAlerts helps you remember birthdays, plan celebrations, and create birthday cards. This policy describes how information is handled by the app.

Information you choose to add

The app lets you save names, birthdays, relationships, notes, gift ideas, and photos. This information is stored on your device. You control what you add and can edit or delete saved entries.

Photos and birthday cards

If you choose to add a photo to a person or card, the app uses that photo to provide the requested feature. If you save a card to Photos, iOS may ask for permission.

If you share a card, the information in that card is shared with the destination you choose. The receiving app or service has its own privacy practices.

Notifications and widgets

If enabled, the app schedules local birthday reminders and may make upcoming birthday information available to its home screen widget. Notification previews and widgets may be visible to people with access to your device, depending on your settings.

Purchases

An optional one-time purchase unlocks unlimited birthdays. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. CakeDayAlerts does not collect your payment card information.

Backups and device data

You can export a backup and choose where to save or share it. Keep backups in a location you trust because they can contain personal information.

Deleting the app may delete locally stored app data unless you have retained a backup.

Advertising and analytics

CakeDayAlerts is designed without third-party advertising or in-app analytics. Apple may separately process App Store transactions and app diagnostics according to Apple's policies.

Children's privacy

The app is a personal birthday organizer and is not designed to collect information directly from children.

Users should obtain any necessary permission before adding or sharing another person's information or photo.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.
